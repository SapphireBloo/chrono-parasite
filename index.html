How to Play

Drain time, evolve, and outlast every other parasite in the arena.

Objective

Stay alive by stealing time from orbs and other parasites. More time = you age from Young → Adult → Elder, unlocking new abilities.

  • Young: Fast, only Tentacle.
  • Adult: Gains Dash.
  • Elder: Gains Shield / Parry.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Primary – Tentacle (LMB): Aim with the mouse, click to latch and drain time.
  • Secondary – Dash (RMB, Adult+): Burst forward. On hit, steal a big chunk of time; on miss, lose time.
  • Shield / Parry (SPACE, Elder): Brief barrier. If an enemy hits you, they lose time and are slowed.

The Chronovore

A segmented worm roams the arena and it does not negotiate. Touching any part of it is an instant kill, and your banked time scatters across the floor as orbs for everyone else.

  • The screen edges bleed red and the camera shakes as it closes in.
  • It picks targets loosely — leaders and nearby parasites are likelier, but it roams too.
  • Watch the red trail on the minimap to read where it's heading.

Wormholes

The spinning rifts are neutral teleporters. Touch one and you're dropped somewhere safe and random. There's a short cooldown before you can use another, so don't count on chaining them.

  • The best panic button when the Chronovore has your scent.
  • The camera tugs toward a rift as you approach — that's your range warning.

Tether Movement

Fire your tentacle at a rock instead of a parasite and you'll anchor to it, gaining a large speed boost while attached. Anyone you bump into while tethered is knocked away and staggered.

Interface

  • Minimap (bottom-left): orbs, rifts, rivals, and the Chronovore's trail.
  • Leaderboard (top-left): live standings — time banked plus kills.
  • Esc: pause. R: restart after death.

Tips

  • Eat orbs early to reach Adult quickly.
  • Use Tentacle at max range to stay safer.
  • Dash is high risk, high reward — use it to finish weakened enemies.
  • Elder shield can turn an enemy’s attack into your advantage.
  • Time is both your health and your score — spending it on abilities is a real gamble.
  • Let the Chronovore thin out the leaders, then feed on the orbs it leaves behind.
